# Surface brief: portfolio home (index.html)

Scope: single-page portfolio with hash-routed full-screen case studies. Mode: Experience (the work leads), with a Persuade action (email / résumé).
Audience: hiring managers and research PIs, equally. Action: open a case study, then email or download the résumé.
Constraints: no invented facts. Résumé wins conflicts. Research (CES, TECO, DataInsight) is separate from industry Experience.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A magazine feature on one engineer, set in large type on white paper. It refuses the newsprint masthead, cream paper and tracked-mono label costume of the previous site and of the category.
OWN-WORLD: White paper (#FFF) and #F1F1F1 panels, ink #111, soft ink #575757, a single ultramarine (#2433FF) that owns the full Contact field and the active filter. Gelasio display (500–700) with Schibsted Grotesk for text. Square corners, no rules, no shadows at rest; surfaces are separated by tone steps.
STORY: The visitor learns who he is (systems from kernel to agents), sees three true figures, opens real case studies, reads the research and industry record, then emails.
FIRST VIEWPORT: "Hamza / Aamer" in two stepped lines at about 12.5vw. The first line is flush left and the second flush right, with the white-ground portrait between them so the letters cross the black shirt and invert. Bottom row: the one-line positioning sentence on the left and "See the work" in solid ink on the right. Wordmark at top left, three links at top right.
FORM: type-only-statement (taste-skill direction, kept with the approved portrait), stats as figures in a sentence, work as staggered tiles with a domain filter, research as giant figures compared, experience as an expandable line-up with a span timeline, about as two columns with highlights, contact as display rows on ultramarine, footer as a full-width wordmark. Signature: masked headline rise, plus View Transition shared-element case-study open. seed: none (unavailable)
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
